## Evidence and limits

`prepare.py` ran actual Yosys `proc`, register D/Q boundary extraction, `techmap`, `opt`, ABC Boolean gate mapping, and formal equivalence checks. It did not call Design Compiler, PrimeTime or generate physical PPA labels. Cell count and combinational logic depth are transparently new structural targets. The sole open counter remains held out.

All training is CPU, from random initialization. No model weights, APIs, external LLM, paid service, account operation, gated agreement, push or publication. Heuristic summaries are identified as such; they are not an LLM proxy claim. CPU setup uses the existing venv and adds about 26.8 MB of advertised wheel payload, plus tiny raw files and metadata. Compiled WASM cache is local and excluded from Git. Exact neural timing/peak RSS is in metrics; tool compilation peak was not separately instrumented.

Shared allocated parameters and identical minibatches/steps/seeds do not imply equal FLOPs: graph removal/text-only activate fewer parameters and omit graph objectives. `shuffled_edges` retains the graph modules/objectives and isolates structural signal more closely. Rewiring is deterministic for seed and minibatch position; a given sample can receive different rewiring when its batch position changes. It preserves node/edge counts, not acyclicity or each named node's degree. These are corrupted-input controls, not valid synthesized alternative circuits.
